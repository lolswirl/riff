ABOUT

Riff puts your Spotify on your glasses: album art, song, artist, album and year, and a live progress line, rendered right on your G2 HUD. Play, pause, skip, and scrub through a song from the touchbar without ever pulling out your phone. Music in your ears, controls on your face, phone in your pocket. Set it up once and it just works. Start a song on any Spotify device and it shows up on your lens.


HOW IT WORKS

Riff works with Spotify Premium. Connect once and every launch after that is instant. Riff's settings walk you through making a free Spotify developer app to get your API key (its client ID) and signing in, all from your phone.

Riff follows whatever you're playing on any Spotify device. The glasses show a full player with real album art dithered onto the lens, the song, the artist, the album and year, and a progress line you can scrub.

After a few quiet moments the player shrinks into the corner of your choice, out of the way. Or let it hide completely for a clear view. It pops back when the song changes or you touch the touchbar.

Make it yours in Settings: show everything in lowercase, flip the swipe directions, or turn off the dots to keep your album art smooth.

Riff is not affiliated with Spotify.


GESTURES

•  tap  →  play / pause (while hidden, the first tap shows the player)
↓  swipe down  →  next song
↑  swipe up  →  previous song
─  hold  →  scrub mode (hold again to cancel, or from the corner, back to the full display)
↕  swipe in scrub mode  →  move the position 5 seconds (bigger steps on quick repeats)
•  tap in scrub mode  →  jump to that spot
••  double-tap  →  exit (asks you to confirm)
